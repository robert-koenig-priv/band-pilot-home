Built for the whole band

One band. Two kinds of seat.

BandPilot is collaboration first. The music lives in one shared place, so nobody argues over which version of the setlist is current — because there's only ever one.

Members · on their phone

Everyone plays from the same page

Each musician installs the app and joins the band. From the couch or the rehearsal room they can look up a song's key and tempo, rate what they want to play, flag what needs work and follow along with the video.

  • Browse the catalog and open any song's details
  • Rate songs and cast a veto to steer the setlist
  • Practise with the built-in A/B loop player
  • See the next rehearsal's plan and who's out
Band admin · runs the system

You keep the show on the road

The admin sets up the band, curates the catalog, promotes songs from Suggested to Ready for stage and builds rehearsal setlists. It all works on the phone, but the roomy web app is the recommended cockpit for admin work.

  • Add songs, set keys, BPM, flags and status
  • Order rehearsals with drag-and-drop setlists
  • Mark absent members and email the band an update
  • Manage several bands from one account
